Priorities & resources needed

Drafted using general small-business best practice — foundational financial/legal footing first, then brand protection and the systems bottlenecks already slowing day-to-day work, then growth initiatives. This is a starting point for the owner to reorder based on real-world urgency, budget, and time.

1
Get real financial statements in place
Nothing downstream — tax/entity decisions, lender or investor conversations, revenue-mix tracking, even judging whether the doubling-volume goal is healthy — can be answered without a P&L, balance sheet, and basic bookkeeping. Lisa is gathering these now.
Critical · NowBookkeeper / fractional CPA
2
Resolve tax & entity structure questions
LLC vs. S-corp election, whether Modern Dad/Teen/DIY become separate entities under the umbrella, employing Lisa's kids & Roth IRAs, and the company-vehicle / personal-vehicle-sale questions. These decisions shape how trademarks and contracts should be filed, so they belong ahead of #3.
Critical · NowCPA + business attorney
3
Protect the brand names — trademark filings
The Modern Mom, Modern Mom, Modern Dad, Modern Teen, and Modern DIY are all in public use with no protection. Risk grows the longer this waits; ideally files under whatever entity structure #2 settles on.
HighIP / trademark attorney
4
Decide on themodernmom.com and related domains
themodernmom.com is currently for sale at $5K, plus the Modern Dad / Teen / DIY domains — a time-boxed, use-it-or-lose-it decision. Low complexity, but needs an explicit budget call soon.
High · Time-boxedOwner budget decision
